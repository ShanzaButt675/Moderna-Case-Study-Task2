# Moderna-Case-Study-Task2
A case study of Moderna as an mRNA biotechnology platform, prepared as an 11-slide presentation. Covers its core mRNA technology, product pipeline, manufacturing approach, market impact, and a SWOT analysis.
As part of my Biotechnology Internship at Code Alpha, I recently completed a case study analysis on Moderna, a leading mRNA biotechnology company. In this task, I examined how Moderna's core innovation works — designing mRNA sequences that instruct cells to temporarily produce a target protein, without altering the genome. I analyzed their product pipeline across vaccines, cancer immunotherapy, and rare-disease treatments, along with their manufacturing model that combines digital mRNA design with automated production. I also evaluated Moderna's market impact and prepared a SWOT analysis to understand the company's strengths, challenges, and future opportunities as a platform-based biotechnology business.
