# Moderna-Case-Study-Task2

A case study analysis of Moderna, a leading mRNA biotechnology company, prepared as part of my Biotechnology Internship at **Code Alpha**.

## Overview

This case study examines Moderna's mRNA platform — how the company designs messenger RNA to instruct cells to produce specific proteins without permanently altering the genome. It analyzes the company's product pipeline, manufacturing model, market performance, and strategic position within the biotechnology industry.

## Key Topics Covered

- Core mRNA platform: design, delivery, manufacturing, and clinical translation
- Commercial products (SPIKEVAX®, mRESVIA®, mNEXSPIKE®)
- Pipeline expansion into oncology and rare-disease therapeutics
- Digital biotechnology and automated manufacturing
- Market impact and commercial performance
- SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
